Generate the full evidence bundle
After exporting the revised draft, slice its helper project and shell-only baseline with the same Orca build and printer, filament and process settings. Turn automatic arrangement and orientation off. On the compute worker, run:
fdmgen evidence REPORT.json project.gcode shell-only.gcode --table TABLE.json --pose POSE_ID --out out/evidence
Replace the placeholders with this export report, its two corresponding G-codes, the exact saved table and selected pose ID. The worker needs the matching part source checkout; for the bracket adapter, use an adjacent checkout or SPOOL_RACK_ROOT.
